# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS, no build step. Deployed to GitHub Pages.

## Users

Producers and directors hiring a Director of Photography (and, secondarily, a gaffer) for short films, fashion films, commercials, music videos and video art. They arrive from a link (Instagram, email, CV), skim the reel and recent credits, and decide whether to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Portfolio of Alberto Alcubilla, Director de Fotografía (DoP) and Gaffer, based in Spain. Success: a producer watches the reel, sees credible recent work, and contacts him by email, phone or Instagram.

## Positioning

A DoP who is also a working gaffer: he lights his own frames and has engineering training (telecom/sound & image) plus audiovisual communication, a DoP master's (TAI) and ARRI certification. Technique and creativity together.

## Operating Context

Visitors mostly open it on a phone from Instagram, or on a laptop when shortlisting crew. Spanish-language audience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: Reel, Works (credits), About, Contact.
- DoP role leads; Gaffer credits are complementary.
- Copy in Spanish; role labels like "Short Film", "Fashion Film" kept as in the original.
- No contact form backend (static hosting): contact via mailto, tel and Instagram links.
